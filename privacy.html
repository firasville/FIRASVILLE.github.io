Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

TCB-Rclone is a private configuration of Rclone used by its owner to access and manage authorized Google Drive accounts.

Google Drive Data

The application requests Google Drive access to browse, upload, download, copy, move, and manage files as directed by the account owner.

Data Storage and Sharing

Google authentication credentials are stored in the owner's local Rclone configuration. Files may be transferred to storage destinations selected and controlled by the owner.

TCB-Rclone does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers.

Data Retention

Transferred files remain in the selected storage locations until removed by the owner. Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
